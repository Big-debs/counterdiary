Methodology

Public Pulse reports are generated from anonymized, aggregated data from THE LEDGER diary platform. We collect:

  • Daily owner diary entries (diesel costs, staff, spoilage, leakage)
  • Anonymous worker reports (wages, conditions, casualization)
  • Customer feedback (portion size, price trust, loyalty)

All data is anonymized at source. No business names, no phone numbers, no IP addresses. Reports are released weekly under Creative Commons — free to share with attribution.
